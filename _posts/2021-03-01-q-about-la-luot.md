## 1. Who is this course for?

Learners who wish to improve their English pronunciation, intonation and speaking fluency.

---

## 2. What will you gain from this course?

- A better understanding of the differences between Vietnamese and English speech.
- An understanding of stress, rhythm, intonation and accent.
- Techniques for improving vocal resonance and practising vocal warm-ups.
- Techniques for chunking and delivering longer passages more fluently.
- Guidance for identifying speaking difficulties and continuing to practise independently.

**Note:** Do not expect your voice to change completely after four sessions. The goal is to equip you with techniques for independent practice. Consistent practice is essential.

---

## 3. How does this course differ from traditional pronunciation teaching?

Traditional approaches often focus on articulatory details and isolated sounds, which can be difficult for beginners to apply in natural speech.

This course focuses on broader features of spoken English, including stress, rhythm and intonation, alongside practical exercises for improving speech.

---

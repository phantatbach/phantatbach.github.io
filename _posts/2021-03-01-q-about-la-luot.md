## 1. Who is this course for?

- Learners who experience difficulties with spoken English unrelated to the content of what they say and wish to improve.
- The course was originally designed for learners without visual, speech or hearing disabilities.

---

## 2. What will you gain from this course?

- A different approach to listening and speaking, including how to identify and correct your own mistakes.
- An understanding of the differences between Vietnamese and English speech, including why native speakers may struggle to understand apparently accurate pronunciation.
- The ability to recognise sounds automatically, useful in everyday communication and examinations.
- Familiarity with English sounds in US and UK varieties and the ability to distinguish difficult sound contrasts.
- 1,000 common vocabulary items (A1–B2) for everyday communication and examinations.
- Techniques for practising natural intonation and delivering longer passages fluently.
- Techniques for developing a more authoritative-sounding voice.

**Note:** Do not expect your voice to change completely immediately after the course. The goal is to equip you with the direction and techniques needed for independent practice in the future. Consistent practice is essential.

---

## 3. How does this course differ from traditional pronunciation teaching?

- Traditional approaches often focus on articulatory details, which can be difficult for beginners:
  - Technical terminology for speech organs can be confusing.
  - Instructions such as raising the tongue, rounding the lips, increasing pitch or speaking more forcefully can be difficult to visualise and follow.
- Traditional approaches often focus on isolated sounds rather than sequences of sounds as they occur in actual speech.

This course simplifies articulatory theory and places greater emphasis on listening and speaking in different contexts to develop familiarity with natural pronunciation.

---

## 4. What is the difference between the Complete and Personal courses?

- **Complete:** Covers all course modules.
  - **Duration:** 16 sessions, to be completed within six weeks.
- **Personal:** Covers only the areas in which you experience difficulties.
  - **Duration:** Up to 11 sessions, to be completed within four weeks.

---

## 5. Why is there a course completion deadline?

- This is an intensive course requiring substantial practice within a short period. Extending the course may reduce its effectiveness by interrupting continuity.
- Course duration also affects my availability.

**Note:** Please plan your schedule to complete the course within the specified timeframe.

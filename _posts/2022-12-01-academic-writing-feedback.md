## 1. Service content

**Scope:** This service is exclusively for university coursework, including academic assignments, projects and dissertations. It does not cover scholarship application documents. For application-related feedback, please refer to [Scholarship Mentoring]({% post_url 2026-11-25-scholarship-mentoring %}).

- Receive feedback on your university assignments, including ideas, organisation, grammar, vocabulary, citations and other aspects of academic writing.
- **Platform:** Email + online meeting platforms.
- **Turnaround time:** 24–72 hours. You will be notified of any exceptional delays.
- You may also book a one-to-one consultation to discuss the feedback.

**Note:** Your writing may be used as an example in my courses, with personal information anonymised.

---

## 2. Fees

- **Written feedback + 1 one-to-one meeting:** 1,000,000 VND per submission (maximum 4,000 words).
- **Supervisory including feedback and (bi)-weekly meetings:** 7,000,000 VND for the whole project/thesis.

---

## 3. Process

After registration, email me your writing. I will return it with detailed comments.

---

## 4. Note

For highly specialised assignments outside my area of expertise, my comments will be primarily advisory and intended to provide general guidance.

## 1. Service content

- Receive feedback on your university and scholarship applications, including ideas, organisation, language and guidance on how to strengthen your applicant profile.
- **Platform:** Email and online meetings.

**Note:** Your application results may be used as examples for my services, with personal information anonymised.

---

## 2. Fees

The document and application form fees cover iterative feedback and one-to-one consultations from registration until the agreed application deadline. Interview preparation is charged separately per session.

- **CV / Résumé:** 3,000,000 VND
- **Statement of Purpose / Letter of Intent / Motivation Letter / Personal Statement:** 3,000,000 VND per document
- **Letter of Recommendation (LOR):** 3,000,000 VND (up to two recommenders)
- **Scholarship Essay:** 3,000,000 VND per document
- **Research Statement / Research Intent:** 3,000,000 VND per document
- **Research Proposal:** 3,000,000 VND
- **Admission / Scholarship Application Form:** 1,500,000–3,000,000 VND per form, depending on the length and complexity of the required responses.
- **Interview Preparation:** 600,000 VND per session

Fees cover feedback on content, structure, argumentation and language, rather than writing documents on behalf of applicants.

## 3. Process

Please email me to discuss your application, mentoring needs and application deadline.

## 4. Note

The depth and subject-specific relevance of my feedback depend on how closely your field aligns with my academic background and expertise.

My order of priority is:

1. Linguistics and STEM
2. Social Sciences and Humanities
3. Arts

The higher the priority of your field, the more closely tailored my feedback is likely to be.

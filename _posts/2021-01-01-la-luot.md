Lả Lướt is a short course focusing on English pronunciation, intonation, speaking fluency and voice clarity.

## 1. Course content

**Module 1: Introduction**

- Course overview
- Language comparison
- Stress
- Rhythm
- Intonation
- Accent

**Module 2: Improving Your Speech**

- Resonance: Techniques for improving your voice.
- Vocal warm-up: Basic vocal warm-up and voice training exercises.
- Chunking: Techniques for delivering longer passages fluently.

**Current course format:** Lả Lướt currently covers only two modules (Introduction and Improving Your Speech), delivered over four sessions for 2,000,000 VND.

---

## 1. Course content

**Module 1: Introduction**
- Course Overview
- Language Comparison
- Stress
- Rhythm
- Intonation
- Accent

**Module 2: Sounds of English**
- Consonants (individual sounds and clusters)
- Vowels (monophthongs and diphthongs)

**Module 3: Accent**
- Practising intonation and accent through films (alongside Module 2).

**Module 4: Connecting the Sounds**
- Connected Speech
- Contractions

**Module 5: Improving Your Speech**
- Resonance: Techniques for improving your voice.
- Vocal Warm-up: Basic vocal warm-up and voice training exercises.
- Chunking: Techniques for speaking fluently when reading longer texts.

**Module 6: The British Accent**
- Features of British dialects (intonation, pronunciation and vocabulary).

### Duration of the full course

16 sessions of approximately 60 minutes each (3–5 sessions per week).

## 2. Entry assessment (Personal course only)

Email your assessment with the subject line: "Full Name – Lả Lướt Assessment".

### 2.1 Introduce yourself

Record an approximately two-minute self-introduction without a script.

### 2.2 Read and record the following

You may look up pronunciations if needed.

> "That quick beige fox jumped in the air over each thin dog. Look out, I shout, for he's foiled you again, creating chaos."

**Sample Paragraph**  
Every year we go to Florida. We like to go to the beach. My favorite beach is called Emerson Beach. It is very long, with soft sand and palm trees. It is very beautiful. I like to make sandcastles and watch the sailboats go by. Sometimes there are dolphins and whales in the water! Every morning we look for shells in the sand. I found fifteen big shells last year. I put them in a special place in my room. This year I want to learn to surf. It is hard to surf, but so much fun! My sister is a good surfer. She says that she can teach me. I hope I can do it!

### 2.3 Identify words from a recording

Repeat the words in the recording and, if possible, write them down.
